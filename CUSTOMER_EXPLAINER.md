# CUSTOMER_EXPLAINER.md — Customer ko SH Ads Desk samjhane ki script

Kisi bhi industry ka customer ho — kirana, real estate, coaching,
clinic, salon, software — usko ye software **kaise connect hoga,
kaise kaam karega, aur uske paise kaise bachayega**, ye is script
se samjhana hai. Bhasha jaan-bujh kar simple rakhi hai: customer ko
system ki andar ki baatein nahi, **Meta Ads ka kaam aur uska fayda**
samajhna hai.

> ⚠️ Bolne me imaandari: "guaranteed result / guaranteed bachat"
> kabhi mat bolo — ye Meta ki policy ke bhi khilaf hai aur system
> ka rule bhi. Bachat ka matlab hai: **bekaar kharch rukna** —
> uske tareeke neeche saaf diye hain, wahi bolo.

---

## Ek line me software kya hai (pehle ye bolo)

> "Ye ek system hai jo aapke Facebook–Instagram ads ka pura kaam
> khud karta hai — competitor research, ad banana, ad chalana,
> jo ad kaam nahi kar raha use rokna, jo kar raha hai use badhana,
> aur roz aapko report dena. Aapko sirf apna budget batana hai."

## Step 1 — Connect kaise hoga (customer ko ye samjhao)

Customer ko sirf **teen cheezein ek baar** karni hain, hum saath
rahenge:

1. **Apna Meta account jodna (ek baar)** — unka apna Facebook
   Page, Business account aur Ad Account system se connect hoga.
   Password hum kabhi nahi maangte — connect Meta ki apni screen
   par hota hai, account **unka hi rehta hai**.
2. **Apne products/services ki list** — kya bechna hai, uski
   sahi jaankari (jo cheez sach me bech sakte ho, sirf wahi ad
   me jayegi).
3. **Budget batana** — roz/mahine kitna kharch karna hai. Yahi
   unka roz ka ekmatra kaam hai — baad me sirf budget badalna/
   ghatana hai.

> Bolne wali line: "Setup ek baar ka hai. Uske baad aapko roz
> kuch nahi karna — system chalega, aapko report milegi."

## Step 2 — Phir kaam kaise karega (roz ka loop, simple shabdon me)

1. **Research** — system aapke competitors ke ads dekhta hai
   (Meta Ad Library se) — kaunsa ad sabse lambe samay se chal
   raha hai, wahi jeet raha hota hai.
2. **Creative banana** — usi jeetne wale pattern se seekh ke
   aapke liye naye poster, carousel aur reel ke creative bante
   hain — copy nahi hote, pattern hota hai, creative aapka
   apna original hota hai.
3. **Jaanch** — har ad live hone se pehle Meta ki policy aur
   39 galtiyon ki list se check hota hai — galat daava (claim)
   wala ad wahi ruk jata hai.
4. **Ad chalna** — sahi structure, sahi audience, aapke budget
   ke andar.
5. **Roz ki nazar** — jo ad paisa kha ke result nahi de raha,
   system use rokne ka kaam karta hai; jo result de raha hai,
   usi par budget badhta hai. Creative thak jaye to naya ban
   jata hai.
6. **Report** — roz report: kitna kharch hua, kitni enquiry/
   sale aayi, ek lead ka kharch kitna pada.

> Bolne wali line: "Aap subah report dekhoge — paisa kahan
> gaya, kya mila, sab saaf. Beech me system apna kaam khud
> karta rahega."

## Step 3 — Paise kaise bachega (ye sabse dhyaan se sunte hain)

Bachat koi jaadu nahi hai — **bekaar kharch rukne** se hoti hai:

1. **Murda ads par paisa nahi jalta** — aksar log haarne wale ad
   ko hafto chalate rehte hain kyunki roz dekhne ka time nahi
   hota. System roz dekhta hai — jo ad target kharch par result
   nahi de raha, wo rukta hai. Yahi sabse badi bachat hai.
2. **Paisa sirf jeetne wale par lagta hai** — budget dheere-
   dheere, niyam se, sirf usi ad par badhta hai jo result de
   raha hai — andhe me teer nahi chalta.
3. **Shuruaat ki mehngi galtiyaan nahi hoti** — galat objective,
   learning phase me baar-baar edit, bahut saare chhote ad set —
   ye galtiyaan naye advertisers ka budget kha jaati hain. System
   me ye 39 guardrails se pehle hi ruk jaati hain.
4. **Account banne ka kharcha bachta hai** — galat claim wala ad
   reject hota hai, baar-baar ho to account hi band ho sakta
   hai. Har ad pehle policy check se guzarta hai — account
   surakshit, to business surakshit.
5. **Agency/media-buyer ki roz ki mehnat ka kharcha** — research,
   creative, roz ki monitoring aur reporting ke liye alag bande
   ya agency rakhne ki jagah ye kaam system karta hai. (Fees ka
   koi pakka number mat bolo — customer apni agency se khud
   compare kar lega.)
6. **Time bachta hai** — roz Ads Manager khol ke ghanto baithna
   khatm. Time bhi paisa hi hai.

> Bolne wali line: "Hum result ki guarantee nahi dete — koi
> sachha banda dega bhi nahi. Hum ye dete hain: aapka paisa
> bekaar jagah jalega nahi, aur jahan lagega wahan aapko roz
> hisaab milega."

## Har industry ke liye ek line (customer ki bhasha me bolo)

| Industry | Ek line |
|---|---|
| Kirana / Grocery | "Aapke ilaake me jo dukaanen ad chala rahi hain, unke best offers dekh ke aapke liye waise hi creative banenge — aur jo ad order nahi la raha, uspar paisa nahi jalega." |
| Real Estate | "Broker/builder ke jo project ads lambe samay se chal rahe hain, unke pattern par aapke project ke creative banenge — enquiry ka kharch roz report me dikhega." |
| Coaching / Education | "Dusre institutes ke winning ads ke pattern par aapke course ke ads — bina 'guaranteed placement' jaise jhoothe daavon ke, jo account ke liye khatarnak hote hain." |
| Clinic / Salon / Local service | "Aas-paas ke competitors kya offer chala rahe hain, system dekhega — aapke liye usi hisaab ke creative, aur booking/enqiry par roz nazar." |
| Software / Digital products | "Kaunsi tool/category ke ads market me jeet rahe hain, unke pattern par creative — aur har product ki permission pehle confirm, taaki account safe rahe." |
| E-commerce | "Jeetne wale product ads ke pattern par naye creative lagatar — thake hue creative apne aap badalte rehte hain." |

## Do sawal jo customer pakka puchega

**"Mera Meta account aur paisa mere paas hi rahega?"**
> "Bilkul. Account aapka, ad ka paisa seedha Meta ko jaata hai —
> system sirf manage karta hai. Paisa humare paas aata hi nahi.
> Aur ek kill switch hai — kuch galat lage, ek click me sab band."

**"Mujhe kuch karna padega kya roz?"**
> "Sirf report dekhni hai aur budget batana hai. Baaki research,
> creative, monitoring — system ka kaam hai. Kuch bada badlav
> hoga to wo bhi aapko bata ke hoga."

## Samjhane ka order (beech me kya-kya bolna hai, isi kram me)

1. **Problem** — "Ads me paisa to lagta hai, par pata nahi chalta
   kaunsa ad kaam kar raha hai, aur roz dekhne ka time kiske
   paas hai?"
2. **System kya karta hai** — Step 2 wala loop, 30 second me.
3. **Connect kitna aasan hai** — Step 1 wali teen cheezein.
4. **Paisa kaise bachega** — Step 3 ke points 1, 2 aur 4 khaas
   taur par.
5. **Control aapke paas** — budget aapka, kill switch, roz report,
   paisa seedha Meta ko.
6. **Shuruaat** — "Ek baar connect kar dete hain, pehli report
   se hi aapko farak dikhega ki paisa kahan ja raha hai."
